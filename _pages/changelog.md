# Changelog

Stay up to date with the latest improvements and fixes to Sposiv, your private place saver.

<br>

### `Latest`
# **Version 1.0**
Welcome to Sposiv - your private place saver! Save and organize your favorite places without any tracking, servers, or accounts. Everything stays on your device.

#### Features
- Save any place with just a tap
- Organize with custom categories
- Add notes and links to your spots
- Share extension for easy saving from other apps
- Works completely offline
- 100% private - no data collection

<br>

## **Version 1.0.3**
**The Share Extension Learned Some Manners**

Saving spots from Safari and other apps just got smoother. The share extension now gets out of your way faster - tap, save, done. No more lingering screens wondering if you actually saved it. (You did. We promise.)

#### The Details You'll Never Notice
- Keyboard now has a Done button when adding notes. Revolutionary? No. Obvious? Yes. Should we have done this sooner? Probably.
- Cleaned up some redundant buttons. If a button doesn't spark joy (or serve a purpose), it's gone.

#### Under the Hood (Where the Magic Happens)
- Made version numbers actually match reality. Turns out showing the right version is kind of important when people are testing your app. Who knew?

**The Promise Remains:** Still private. Still no account. Still just mapping your world, your way.

<br>

## **Version 1.0.2**
**Fixed the Thing You Didn't Know Was Broken**

Tutorial highlights now actually align with what they're highlighting. Turns out being off by a few pixels was bothering us more than anyone else.

#### What's New
- Polished the invisible - Bottom navigation got some love. You won't notice, which means we did it right.
- Squashed bugs that were too embarrassed to show themselves - They know what they did.

**The real update:** We're constantly making Sposiv better behind the scenes. No flashy features this time, just the app working exactly how you expect it to. Which, honestly, is the best feature of all.

Still 100% private. Still no account needed. Still just yours.

<br>
